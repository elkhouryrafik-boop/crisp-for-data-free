## Notes on citation discipline

Every claim made in `SKILL.md` and the other references files can be traced to one of these sources. Where a number is given (e.g. "60–87% of data-science projects fail"), the source chain runs through Data Science PM (2024), which compiles the Gartner (2017), VentureBeat (2019), and NewVantage Partners surveys. Where a method is named (e.g. SMART, Five Whys, pre-mortem, Heilmeier), the primary or canonical source is cited above.

The CRISP-DM 1.0 step-by-step guide (Chapman et al. 2000) remains the authoritative source for the four-task / twelve-output reference model. CRISP-ML(Q) (Studer et al. 2021) is the source for the cancellation-criterion requirement and the quality-assurance overlay. Gebru et al. (2021) and Mitchell et al. (2019) are the sources for the product card structure.
